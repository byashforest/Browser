a little note

I've always been fascinated by the nuances of movement. How things move, how moving feels.

The way waves build, swell, and crash. The feeling of momentum shearing into friction. The difference between resisting gravity and yielding into it. Each movement is a negotiation of forces.

Complex and also fleeting. There and then not.

I've wondered for years what it would be like to keep a gesture outside of a body. And be able to re-expereince how it moved.

This fall, I took Tools for Creativity at the School for Poetic Computation. It seemed like a good time to try.

And so, Under.

— Ash Forest
